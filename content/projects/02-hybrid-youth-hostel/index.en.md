Chifeng Street, near the site, is a street of classic character also known as 『歹鐵仔街』, the antiquated-ironware street. I took its industrial elements as the starting point: just as a wrench and a screw fit together, the design brings public, historical and local values into one building, and pulls them close enough for hostel guests to experience them first-hand.
:::

::drawing{src="02-analysis.jpg" caption="Massing development, site analysis and concept"}

An X-shaped geometry creates a connected central space where circulation keeps diverging and converging. Staggered balconies and corridors let wind and sunlight into the building, and a semi-outdoor corridor adds a second skin that protects privacy and keeps out some of the heat.
