The site is defined as an exhibition center closely linked to hydrology. Sitting between a river and a lake, it becomes a transition between the two, using interactions with water to create classification and variation, and giving the public a space to experience the ecosystem closely.

The river system is brought into the site along with the flow of people, creating connected water spaces indoors and out. A central water plaza and a water stage gather visitors and take advantage of the landscape. The project aims to strengthen the connection between people and hydrology, and to raise awareness of the site’s hydrological ecology by combining landscape, performance and water.
