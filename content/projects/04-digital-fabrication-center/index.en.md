Nature and interaction between people can soften the busy atmosphere of traffic and technology. The idea is a set of bridges that connect different functions. As the river flows through the green belt, people wander through it too, in a clear and logical way that won’t get them lost. Stepping outside becomes a chance to find new ideas or simply slow down.

::drawing{src="01-site-concept.jpg" caption="Site analysis and concept"}

Three blocks make up three systems. Bridges between them build up the flow of people, create gaps where people meet, and connect the center to the neighbouring research institutions.
