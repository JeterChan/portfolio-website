:::text-right{src="01-dusk.jpg" alt="The bamboo structure against the dusk sky" caption="At dusk"}
Starting with the exploration of “modern bamboo structures”, we looked for construction methods and industrial components that show the unique working properties and mechanics of bamboo, presenting the nature of bamboo as it is found in the wild.

The experience we imagined is like riding the Alishan Forest Railway: swaying gently back and forth and climbing, almost without noticing, into the misty peaks.
:::

The bamboo, baked and bent, is suspended in the air in a streamlined form with unfinished edges, a blurred reflection of the birds, fish and drifting clouds of the mountain forest. Metal buckles secure the structure in a zigzag “之” pattern that traces a mountain path.
