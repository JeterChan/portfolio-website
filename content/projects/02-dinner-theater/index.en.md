:::text-left{src="01-entrance.jpg" alt="Rendering of the terraced entrance" caption="Terrace and stage"}
The site is defined as a complex of a music square, restaurants and a theater. Through “sonic seasoning”, music and food are fused so the public can experience the space in rich and varied ways.

The project extends Taichung’s leisurely pace of life, brings together the brewery’s food culture and Taichung’s markets, and strengthens dining in the park. Food, music and performance season one another, creating an interface between the four areas around the site and a resonance that engages all five senses.
:::

::drawing{src="02-about-x-space.jpg" caption="About X space: site context and programme"}

::drawing{src="03-concept.jpg" caption="Concept, massing development and traffic analysis"}

The building is divided into four spatial functions: dining, terrace, plaza and theater. The mass is penetrated and set back to create the flow through the space, and an outdoor staircase connects the terraces, the theater and the restaurant.
