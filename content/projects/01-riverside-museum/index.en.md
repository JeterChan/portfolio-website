Placeholder text. The site sits behind a six-metre flood wall that cuts the neighbourhood off from the water. The project treats the wall as a found structure: galleries are hung from it, and a public stair climbs over it to the river.

:::text-left{src="02-model.jpg" alt="Massing model in basswood" caption="Massing model, 1:500"}
Early massing studies tested how many volumes the wall could carry before it stopped reading as a wall. Five volumes kept the line legible from the far bank.

Each gallery is lit from one side only, taking its light from the river.
:::

::drawing{src="03-plan.png" caption="Ground floor plan 1:200"}

The ground floor is left almost entirely open. Workshops and a café line the street edge; the galleries above are reached by one continuous ramp.
